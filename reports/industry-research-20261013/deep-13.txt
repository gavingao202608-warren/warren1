Sign-ups give GDPR consent, tell the team which legal topics they have real experience with, and share a few attributes about themselves. The panel then lives in Formbricks and is queried directly when a study needs participants.The part that made it fast to implement in a public-sector context was first-party data ownership and a comprehensive API. That is exactly where the team used to lose visibility into whether the service actually worked.Now a Formbricks survey sits at the end of the application flow: may we ask you in a few weeks how your experience with legal aid went? Applicants opt in with their email address, which is stored in StackIT. Weeks later, n8n sends the follow-up survey. Incoming anonymous answers are routed straight into a Slack channel via webhook, and the success variables are pulled into a Metabase dashboard alongside product analytics, so the team sees the whole picture in one place.A monthly survey to participating courts closes the loop from the other side, capturing application data quality and how cases were actually decided.UsingLink SurveysWebhooksHidden FieldsREST API0315 Ministry Projects, Strictly Separated, on One InstanceDigitalService works as a contractor: the products and the data belong to the ministries.
