Are you a business or a non-profit organization? Car Help Canada is a non-profit organization. Our mandate is to help and protect consumers when dealing with the auto industry. We also work with auto industry stakeholders to expand consumer protection rights. What can we discuss during a consultation call? We can help you with anything related to car buying. We can discuss different car options, review dealer ads or offers, determine how to best negotiate with the dealer, trade-in, financing, insurance and warranty advice. It’s your time with an industry expert! How can I schedule my consultation session? You will see instructions on your account page. You can schedule a call on the Google Calendar by picking an available time slot, or you can call and leave a message. How should I prepare for my consultation? Understand your needs and budget, prepare your questions, and send us any documents, ads, or dealer offers by email beforehand. Have a way to take notes. Can I have a follow-up call or book a second consultation session? You can purchase an additional session through your account page if necessary. Each consultation session does include up to three follow-up emails with your expert. What is an invoice price quote? An invoice price quote is an itemized price breakdown for a brand-new car model. It includes the mandatory new car charges, rebates, and a dealer discount. It also lets you compare your price to the dealer cost, or invoice price, so you know exactly how much profit the dealer is making on the deal — giving you a baseline to compare against the price the dealer is offering and negotiate a fair deal. See a sample invoice price quote (PDF). Can I have more than one invoice price quote? The membership includes one invoice price quote. If you would like additional quotes to compare the prices of different models, additional quotes can be purchased for $25 plus tax. How much money can I save buying through the Car Help recommended dealership? The Car Help recommended dealership will honour the prenegotiated price quote we prepare for you. This price only includes mandatory new car charges, any available rebates, and an additional dealer discount. The discount varies by model, but the average member saves about $2,000 compared to the regular dealer price. At the very least, you will know you are paying a fair price with no unnecessary fees or markups. Car Help recommended dealers are currently located in the Greater Toronto Area only. Do you help with financing or leasing? Yes. The dealership we refer you to can calculate your payment options for financing or leasing based on the prenegotiated price quote we prepare and the terms you wish to go with. Do you help with extra products and accessories? Products such as vehicle accessories, extended warranty plans, rust protection, and so on can be purchased through the dealership. The dealerships we work with will try to offer discounted pricing on eligible products, but we do not negotiate these as part of the quote we prepare. Do you help with used cars? We can answer any questions you have about used cars during the consultation session. We can also review dealer ads or offers shared with us by email so we can provide feedback. We do not provide price quotes or dealer referrals for used cars. Does your service work across Canada and the USA? The Car Help consultation works across Canada and the USA. The Car Help membership works across Canada; however, we can only provide a recommended dealership for the Greater Toronto Area. How long does the membership last? You have one year to use the services included in the membership. Does the membership auto-renew automatically? No, your membership will not auto-renew. You will receive an email notification two weeks before expiry which gives you the option to renew if you wish to do so. Is my personal data secure? Yes. We only collect enough information to contact and assist you, and to grant access to the online services. Any data is stored in Canadian data centres. We do not store credit card information — only Moneris sees it in order to process a one-time payment. Can I cancel or get a refund? Please see our terms and conditions for full details on our refund policy. Still have a question? We’re happy to help.
