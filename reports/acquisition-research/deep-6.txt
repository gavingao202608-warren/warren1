Where do you operate? I'm based in Toronto and work with clients across Canada, though so far the work has been concentrated in Ontario and the GTA. Sourcing cars from the U.S. or overseas (for delivery into Canada) is something I can take on; I'll quote it honestly based on the specific car and timeline. How long does the whole process take? It depends on the car. Common, in-stock vehicles can close within a week or two of my first call. Rare or imported cars can take longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline before you commit, and I'll tell you up front if the timeline isn't realistic. What if my chosen car needs to be imported? Imports (JDM wagons, European-spec rarities, US-only trims) are outside the two standard services. Can you negotiate on my behalf at the dealership? Yes — that's New Car Negotiation, $499 flat CAD. I canvass dealers, run the price, strip the F&I junk, and hand you a signed-ready quote. The lease-vs-buy math is its own conversation; I'll walk you through which makes more sense for your situation, then negotiate the lease terms (capitalized cost, money factor, residual) on your behalf if leasing wins. What happens if I don't like the cars on the shortlist? The Match includes one round of revisions. If after that none of the suggested cars genuinely fit, you tell me and I refund the $99 in full. I'd rather take an extra round or own the mistake than push you into a car that doesn't fit. Is there a satisfaction guarantee? Both services have one.
